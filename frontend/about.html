About Tehreer

We help people learn from video lectures by turning them into readable notes in the script they use every day.

Why we built it

A lot of excellent teaching in Urdu and Hindi lives in long YouTube videos, and most people in Pakistan read and type Roman Urdu on their phones daily. Taking notes from a two-hour lecture by hand is slow, and a short AI summary throws away exactly the examples and explanations that make a lecture useful.

So we built a tool that keeps the whole lecture. It writes out what the instructor said in English or Roman Urdu (your choice), organises it like a course book, and links every part back to the original video.

Our principles

Faithful over clever

We check word counts and retry instead of trusting a single pass.

Visible progress

You can see what the tool is doing, chunk by chunk, with full logs.
