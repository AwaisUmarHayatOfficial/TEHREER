Every sentence stays

Each chunk of the video is converted sentence by sentence. The output is checked by counting words against the source, and any chunk that comes back too short is retried or split into smaller parts and converted again.

Common questions

Is it free?

The tool is free to use. It runs on your own Google Gemini API key, which has a free tier subject to Google's quotas and rate limits. Check Google's current pricing for details.

Do you store my API key?

No. The key is sent to the server for your job, passed to the conversion process and used only to call Google's Gemini API. It is not written to disk or to logs. If you tick “Remember the key in this browser”, it is saved in your own browser's local storage only, and you can clear it any time by unticking the box and starting a new job.

Which languages can I choose?

English or Roman Urdu. You pick one on the tool page before each conversion. English notes are written in clear English even if the lecture is in Urdu or Hindi, and Roman Urdu notes are written in Roman Urdu (Urdu in Latin letters).

Which videos work?

Videos with Urdu, Hindi or English captions, manual or automatic. Videos with captions disabled cannot be converted.
